## 3. Market Background & Official Baltic Specification

As established in [`SIH26006_Blueprint (1).md`](file:///c:/Users/win11/Downloads/sih/SIH26006_Blueprint%20%281%29.md) (§9.1, §15.1, §17.1):

1. **Standard Representative Vessel (BPI 82)**:
   - **Vessel Class:** Panamax / Kamsarmax (Gearless Bulk Carrier)
   - **Reference Deadweight (DWT):** 82,500 mt on 14.43 m Summer Salt Water (SSW) draft
   - **Length Overall (LOA):** 229.0 m
   - **Beam:** 32.25 m
   - **Speed & Consumption:** 13.5 knots laden on 33 mt IFO / 12 knots ballast on 24 mt IFO
   - **TPC (Tonnes per Centimetre Immersion):** 70.5 t/cm
2. **Benchmark Basket Evolution**:
   - Until January 2026: The historical Panamax benchmark was based on the **4TC** (74,000 dwt reference).
   - In January 2026: The Baltic Exchange completed the migration of open interest to the **P5TC** (82,500 dwt Kamsarmax reference) and officially discontinued P4TC on 30 January 2026.
   - **Weight in BDI:** The BPI accounts for **30%** of the composite Baltic Dry Index (BDI = $0.40 \times \text{BCI} + 0.30 \times \text{BPI} + 0.30 \times \text{BSI}$).

## 4. Landscape of External Data Sources & Provenance Restrictions

A thorough live web investigation into Baltic Exchange BPI historical data yielded the following provenance constraints:

1. **Official Baltic Exchange (`balticexchange.com`)**:
   - Access to historical daily assessments requires an authorized member login or commercial API license.
   - The public website protects market data behind a cryptographic validation challenge (`Challenge Validation`), preventing unauthenticated scraping.
2. **Academic Repositories (Zenodo / Harvard Dataverse / Mendeley Data)**:
   - Academic replication packages (e.g., Zenodo record for *"The Grapes of Delay: The Changing Drivers of Global Food Prices"*, August 2026) explicitly state that the **Baltic Panamax Index is provider-controlled proprietary data** and cannot be redistributed. Researchers provide Stata code templates rather than raw series.
3. **Financial Data Aggregators (Investing.com / MacroMicro / TradingView)**:
   - **Investing.com**: Index ticker `BPNI`. Historical tabular view is protected by Cloudflare bot protection (HTTP 403 Forbidden) and Terms of Service strictly forbid automated scraping.
   - **MacroMicro**: Provides charts, but bulk CSV export of index series requires a paid "Max AI" subscription.
   - **TradingView**: Tracks `INDEX-BDI` (composite BDI), but does **not** carry an official public ticker for BPI (the acronym `BPI` on TradingView resolves to Bank of the Philippine Islands or Bitcoin ETFs).
   - **`yfinance`**: Does not provide coverage for Baltic Exchange indices (`^BDIY` is not hosted by Yahoo Finance).
4. **GitHub Open Source Repositories**:
   - Extensive code search reveals that public CSV files with column headers like `BPI` are predominantly **Bridgepoint Education** stock quotes, **Banco BPI** transactions, or **SMDG Terminal Codes** (Bridgetown Port Inc.), rather than Baltic Exchange data.
   - Open source discussions (e.g. `koala73/worldmonitor`) caution against regex-scraping broker blogs due to transcription errors, lags, and Terms of Service violations.
